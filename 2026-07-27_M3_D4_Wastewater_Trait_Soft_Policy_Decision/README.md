# M3-D4 wastewater-trait soft-policy decision

Date: 2026-07-27  
Status: `T1 SELECTED / ALL TRAITS SOFT / M4B AND M4C LOCKED`

The biological meeting selected the all-soft D4 policy:

- retain wastewater-relevant trait information for user reference, advice,
  explanation and uncertainty;
- do not automatically delete microorganisms;
- do not apply hard rejection;
- do not inherit traits between species and strain.
